# InspirQ Energy Yellow Color System

## Brand intent

The rebrand balances four psychological signals important to an energetic, accessible sports platform:

- **Energy yellow** communicates motion, optimism, visibility, and the spark that gets people onto the field. It is the unmistakable InspirQ brand color.
- **Dark performance ink** is paired with yellow on controls and brand surfaces so labels and icons remain readable at WCAG AA contrast levels.
- **Champagne gold** signals achievement, quality, and reward. It remains reserved for trophies, premium moments, and selective emphasis.
- **Warm ivory** reduces the clinical feeling of pure gray canvases and creates a hospitality-led, editorial surface for venue, coach, and event discovery.
- **Mineral ink and warm neutrals** improve hierarchy and perceived craftsmanship while keeping dense operational interfaces calm.

## Semantic roles

- Primary actions, active navigation, focus, and high-energy brand moments use the yellow scale.
- Achievement and premium accents use champagne; champagne is not used for long-form text or primary controls.
- Information uses muted sapphire, warnings use burnished amber, errors use restrained ruby, and success uses jade.
- Public discovery and role dashboards share the same canvas, borders, and text hierarchy to keep the product coherent across audiences.

## Accessibility posture

- Primary yellow with dark performance ink is used for action contrast; white text is not placed on yellow controls.
- Body copy uses mineral ink rather than gold or muted color.
- Status never relies on hue alone; labels, icons, and text remain present.
- Focus rings remain visually distinct from both canvas and controls.
- Success and verified states use accessible sport blue, errors remain red, and warning states use amber. No product-owned state competes with Energy Yellow.
